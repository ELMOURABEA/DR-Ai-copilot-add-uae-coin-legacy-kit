## 🔗 Digital Legacy & NFT Twin

Each physical coin is linked to a unique NFT (Non-Fungible Token) on the blockchain:

- **Immutable proof of ownership:** Enhanced collectibility and digital provenance
- **Microsite Integration:** The coin's QR code links directly to a dedicated official microsite (gov.ae) for verification, historical content, and future engagements
- **Empowering Youth:** A digital portal inspiring the next generation with UAE's legacy of innovation
