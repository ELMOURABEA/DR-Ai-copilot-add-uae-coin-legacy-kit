# Quick Reference Guide - Review & Publishing Process

A quick reference for the review and publishing workflow for the UAE 1972-2025 Commemorative Coin Legacy Kit.

## 📝 Before You Publish - Essential Steps

### Step 1: Complete the Review Checklist ✅
Open and complete: **[REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md](REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md)**

Key areas to check:
- [ ] Content accuracy (English & Arabic)
- [ ] Technical specifications
- [ ] Cultural appropriateness
- [ ] Government compliance
- [ ] Security & privacy

### Step 2: Follow Publishing Guidelines 📋
Review and follow: **[PUBLISHING_GUIDELINES.md](PUBLISHING_GUIDELINES.md)**

Process overview:
1. Content development & internal review
2. Quality assurance & validation
3. Cultural & compliance review
4. Approval process (multiple reviewers)
5. Submission to government channels

### Step 3: Get Required Approvals ✍️
Obtain sign-offs from:
- Content Reviewer
- Technical Reviewer
- Cultural Advisor
- Legal Reviewer (if applicable)
- Senior Manager

### Step 4: Prepare Submission Package 📦
Gather all materials:
- Project documentation
- Design files and images
- Certificate templates
- Technical specifications
- Bilingual presentations

### Step 5: Submit Through Official Channels 📤
Use approved submission methods:
- Official UAE government portal
- Designated government email
- Physical delivery (if required)

## 🤝 Contributing

Want to contribute? Follow these steps:
1. Read **[CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md)**
2. Create an issue to discuss your proposal
3. Fork and create a branch
4. Make changes following guidelines
5. Complete self-review checklist
6. Submit pull request
